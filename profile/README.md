## Slim Boost Tea Side Effects and Safety

The formula deserves more safety attention than the phrase "100% natural" might suggest.

**Senna:** Senna is a stimulant laxative. Frequent or continued use can cause dependence and interfere with normal bowel activity.

**Green tea:** Green tea beverages are generally considered safe for adults, but they contain caffeine. Concentrated green tea extracts have also been associated with adverse effects, including rare liver injury.

**Garcinia cambogia:** NCCIH reports numerous cases of liver toxicity associated with garcinia products.

**Licorice root:** Large or prolonged amounts of glycyrrhizin-containing licorice can cause serious problems, including abnormal heart rhythm. People with hypertension, heart or kidney conditions may be particularly vulnerable.

**Ginseng:** Asian ginseng can cause insomnia and may interact with medicines. NCCIH recommends discussing herbal products with a healthcare professional when medications are involved.

**Cinnamon and dandelion:** Evidence for weight loss is unclear, and both can have safety or interaction considerations at supplemental amounts.

Because exact quantities of the Slim Boost Tea ingredients are not consistently published, the safety profile of the finished formula cannot be determined simply from the ingredient names.

Pregnant or breastfeeding individuals, people with chronic conditions, and anyone taking prescription medicines should consult a qualified healthcare professional before using the product.
